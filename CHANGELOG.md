# Changelog

## v0.5.0-beta.1 - Beta 0.5.0

Local multiplayer and split screen: 756 additional original-ROM functions regenerated from the user's ROM (guest definitions 3,759 to 4,537), plus a per-player input assignment (keyboard and mouse, gamepads, VR) under PC OPTIONS → PLAYERS or `input-assignment-v1.ini`. Accepted desktop and VR improvements since 0.2.1 are on by default: native first-person view and movement, smoother animation, sampler and text-colour rendering fixes, higher-refresh pacing, a revised VR layer and a restructured options menu. See RELEASE_NOTES.md for exact evidence and limits.

## v0.2.1-beta.1 - Beta 0.2.1

Scoped repair to locally selected resource-decoder callbacks associated with the reported results-opening crash; includes nine Save-path call repairs to existing original-ROM bodies. Builds and targeted automated checks passed; intermittent gameplay reproduction and player confirmation remain pending. No claim that all intermittent crashes are fixed.

## v0.2.0-beta.1 - Beta 0.2.0

Timer-wrap pickup correction; accepted Syra, reported Warclubs and post-mission Quit fixes; shared black splash transitions; consumer CLI cleanup and VR recenter preservation. See RELEASE_NOTES.md for exact evidence and limits.

## v0.1.0-beta.1 - initial beta

Initial Windows desktop and experimental OpenXR release. Original downloads and release notes remain at https://github.com/ArtfulRascal8/rage-wars-recompiled/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.1.
