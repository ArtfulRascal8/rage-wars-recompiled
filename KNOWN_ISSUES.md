# Beta 0.2.0 limitations

- Start screen: press Escape; Enter and Space do not advance it.
- Power-ups, every item/map, complete campaign/menu coverage and all prior-fix regressions have not been demonstrated on the final executable.
- Quest 3 through Virtual Desktop and broader headset/runtime combinations have not been retested on this release executable. VR requires alternate weapon models and motion controls enabled; calibrated models require user-supplied files. No standalone Quest build is supplied.
- VR attachment, near-wall behavior, moving-shot impacts/damage and native fallback weapons remain experimental.
- Fresh-profile persistence and copied Beta 0.1 profile upgrade coverage are limited to checks actually listed in the release notes. Back up your existing profile before updating.
- Eight unresolved dynamic destinations and original worker PI-register support remain broader coverage leads; no complete playthrough is claimed.
- Interactive volume changes and physical audio-device removal/recovery need wider coverage.
- Independent-machine or donor-denying OS reconstruction remains untested; local manifest/build-input checks do not establish OS isolation.

See RELEASE_NOTES.md for exact executable evidence and earlier owner acceptance.
